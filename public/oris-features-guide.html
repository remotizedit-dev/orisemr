1. Platform Architecture & Role Hierarchy Foundation

ORIS EMR is structured as a high-security, multi-tenant cloud application. Each dental chamber operates in strict tenant isolation, with its own operational schedules, doctor rosters, patient records, catalog procedures, and letterhead configurations.

🏢 Superadmin (Platform Owner)
Manages chamber onboardings at /platform. Controls clinic subscription tiers, feature flags, global doctor catalogs, and tenant suspension status.
🩺 Tenant Admin (Chamber Owner / Lead Dentist)
Full ownership of the chamber: configs clinic logo, working hours, doctor personal schedules, physical chair count, letterhead margins, and staff user logins.
👨‍⚕️ Clinical Dentist (Doctor)
Access to in-chair queue, patient history, FDI tooth chart, Bangla prescription builder, in-chair radiograph Lightbox, and confidential doctor's private clinical notes.
👩‍💼 Front Desk / Receptionist
Rapid barcode card scanning, new patient registration, appointment booking, chair queue management, bill settlement, payment collection, and dues recording.

2. The 5-Phase End-to-End Clinic Workflow Core Journey

Content creators should anchor their video around this smooth, friction-free journey showing a patient from arrival to billing.

1

Patient Check-In & Barcode Identification (Reception)

Patient enters chamber. Receptionist scans the physical chamber card barcode (or searches by mobile number). System instantly opens the record, confirms active appointment, and places them into the Live Waiting Queue.

2

Live TV Waiting Lounge & Audio Chime

Lobby TV screen displays the live queue with estimated waiting times. When the dentist clicks "Call to Chair", a pleasant audio chime sounds, guiding the patient to their designated Dental Chair (Chair 1, Chair 2, etc.).

3

In-Chair Consultation & Radiograph Lightbox (Doctor Workspace)

Dentist accesses patient dossier right from the dental chair. Inspects previous X-rays and OPGs via the built-in Lightbox (with 360° rotation and zoom). Checks drug allergies (Penicillin, Sulfa) flagged prominently in red.

4

Sub-60-Second Bangla Prescription Issuance

Doctor clicks affected teeth on the FDI Two-Digit Dental Arch, selects diagnosis via typeahead autocomplete, picks catalog medicines with Bangla dosage templates, writes confidential internal notes, and prints the official prescription.

5

Automated Settlement & Billing (Whole-BDT)

Patient automatically moves from in-chair to Billing. Line items for performed procedures are auto-populated. Cashier settles full or partial balance (Cash/bKash), records previous dues, and prints a branded receipt with barcode.

3. Clinical Prescription Builder Deep Dive Dentist Favorite

🦷 FDI Dental Arch (32-Tooth Selector)
  • Upper Right (11-18), Upper Left (21-28)
  • Lower Left (31-38), Lower Right (41-48)
  • Multi-select teeth with visual highlighted badges
  • Prints explicitly on patient prescription paper
⚡ Typeahead Clinical Autocomplete
  • C/C: Toothache, Cavity, Bleeding gum
  • O/E: Deep caries, Percussion positive
  • Diagnosis: Irreversible pulpitis, Gingivitis
  • Investigations: IOPA X-Ray, OPG Panoramic
🔒 Doctor's Private Clinical Notes (Confidential)

A high-demand feature added specifically for dentists. Doctors can type internal thoughts, diagnostic suspicions, or procedural notes that are strictly stored in the EMR.

Security Guarantee: This note is visible in the internal EMR via the "View" modal, but is never printed onto the patient's paper prescription or receipt.

Drug Allergy Safety Radar

When prescribing, the system cross-references patient allergies (e.g. Penicillin, NSAIDs) with the active ingredient and drug class of catalog medicines in real-time.

⚠️ Warning Notice: Displays an alert banner when prescribing a medication with mild or moderate potential cross-reactivity.
🛑 Hard Allergy Block: Halts prescription submission if prescribing a known severe allergen, requiring intentional override logging.

Bangla Advice & Post-Op Guidelines

Pre-configured standard advice blocks for extraction, RCT, scaling, and orthodontic care that paste into the prescription in 1 click (e.g., তুলা ১ ঘণ্টা শক্ত করে চেপে রাখবেন, গরম বা শক্ত খাবার খাবেন না).

4. Precision Scheduling & Online Booking Anti-Collision

ORIS EMR replaces manual appointment notebooks with a mathematical slot calculation engine that prevents double-booking and wasted chair time.

⚙️ Dynamic Slot Engine Algorithm
  • Split Shifts Support: Morning Shift (10:00 AM - 02:00 PM) & Evening Shift (05:00 PM - 09:00 PM). Automatically blocks lunch/break windows.
  • Dynamic Duration by Procedure: Tooth Extraction (30 mins), Scaling (45 mins), Consultation (15 mins). Timeslots adapt based on selected procedure combo.
  • Buffer Spaces: Configurable 5-15 minute sterilization buffer between consecutive appointments.
  • Real-time Clock Check: When booking for today, slots earlier than current time are automatically removed.

Public Booking Portal (/book/[tenantSlug])

🆕 New Patient Online Booking
Patients booking online enter Name and Phone. The system automatically registers them into the EMR with a collision-proof digital card sequence. They show up across all appointment lists and queues with full name and patient card!
💳 Existing Cardholders Auto-Fill
Existing patients simply enter their Chamber Card ID. The system instantly verifies their record, auto-populates their full name and mobile phone, and confirms their booking in seconds.
Public Waiting Lounge (/lounge/[tenantSlug]): A dedicated URL designed to run fullscreen on a TV in the clinic waiting area, showing currently serving token numbers, patient initials, and upcoming queue.
